# Singapore HDB Build-To-Order (BTO) & Housing Grants Knowledge Base

## 1. Overview of HDB Flat Classification (Standard, Plus, Prime)
From the October 2024 BTO sales launch onwards, HDB implemented a new classification framework replacing mature vs. non-mature estate labels:

### Standard Flats
- **Location:** Islandwide in standard locations with good amenities.
- **Subsidies:** Standard market subsidies.
- **Minimum Occupation Period (MOP):** 5 years before owners can sell the flat on the open market or rent out the whole flat.
- **Subsidy Recovery / Clawback:** None.
- **Renting Out Whole Flat:** Permitted after 5-year MOP.
- **Resale Buyer Eligibility:** Standard resale conditions apply (no additional income ceilings for resale buyers).

### Plus Flats
- **Location:** Choicer locations within regions (e.g., near MRT stations, town centres).
- **Subsidies:** Higher subsidies than Standard flats to keep prices affordable.
- **Minimum Occupation Period (MOP):** 10 years before owners can sell on the open market.
- **Subsidy Recovery / Clawback:** Owners must return a percentage of the resale price (or valuation, whichever is higher) upon selling the flat on the open market. The clawback percentage is pegged to the additional subsidy provided at purchase.
- **Renting Out Whole Flat:** Never permitted (owners may only rent out individual bedrooms even after the 10-year MOP).
- **Resale Buyer Eligibility:** Future buyers on the resale market must meet BTO-like eligibility conditions:
  - At least one Singapore Citizen buyer.
  - Monthly household income ceiling of $14,000 for families, or $7,000 for singles.
  - Private property ownership rule (no private property owned in past 30 months).

### Prime Flats
- **Location:** Prime and central locations (e.g., Greater Southern Waterfront, Queenstown, Bukit Merah, Kallang/Whampoa).
- **Subsidies:** Highest subsidies provided by the government.
- **Minimum Occupation Period (MOP):** 10 years before selling.
- **Subsidy Recovery / Clawback:** Highest subsidy recovery percentage (typically 6% to 12% of resale price/valuation) upon first resale.
- **Renting Out Whole Flat:** Never permitted (only spare bedrooms may be rented out).
- **Resale Buyer Eligibility:** Strict conditions similar to BTO:
  - At least one Singapore Citizen buyer.
  - Monthly household income ceiling of $14,000 for families, or $7,000 for singles.
  - Private property waiting period of 30 months.

## 2. HDB BTO Flat Types & Space Details
- **2-Room Flexi (36 sqm or 45 sqm):** 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, kitchen, storeroom/bomb shelter. Available on 99-year lease for first-timer/second-timer families and singles (age 35+), or on short leases (15 to 45 years) for seniors aged 55 and above.
- **3-Room (approx. 60–65 sqm):** 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, kitchen, living/dining area, bomb shelter.
- **4-Room (approx. 90 sqm):** 3 bedrooms (1 master with ensuite bathroom, 2 common bedrooms), 1 common bathroom, kitchen, service yard, bomb shelter.
- **5-Room (approx. 110 sqm):** 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, kitchen, living/dining area, service yard, bomb shelter, potential study space.
- **3Gen Flats (approx. 115 sqm):** Specially designed for multi-generational families (parents and adult children living together). Features 4 bedrooms (2 master bedrooms with ensuite bathrooms, 2 common bedrooms), 3 bathrooms in total.

## 3. Eligibility Schemes for BTO Flats

### A. Public Scheme
- **Eligibility:** Form a family nucleus with at least one Singapore Citizen (SC) and at least one other SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR).
- **Family Nucleus Options:**
  - You and your spouse (and children, if any).
  - You, your parents, and siblings (if any).
  - You and your children (if widowed or divorced with legal custody).
- **Age Requirement:** At least 21 years old.
- **Household Income Ceiling:** $16,000 monthly household income for families (up to $21,000 for extended/multi-generation families for 3Gen flats).

### B. Fiancé / Fiancée Scheme
- **Eligibility:** An unmarried couple intending to marry.
- **Requirements:**
  - At least one applicant is a Singapore Citizen, and the partner is an SC or SPR.
  - Both applicants must be at least 21 years old.
  - Must register marriage within 3 months of taking key collection for the flat.
- **Household Income Ceiling:** $16,000 monthly household income.

### C. Single Singapore Citizen Scheme
- **Eligibility:** Unmarried Singapore Citizens, divorcees, or widows/widowers.
- **Age Requirement:** Must be at least 35 years old.
- **Eligible Flat Types:**
  - Can buy new 2-Room Flexi BTO flats in Standard, Plus, or Prime projects across all estates.
- **Income Ceiling:** Gross monthly income ceiling is $7,000 for buying a 2-room Flexi BTO flat.

### D. Joint Singles Scheme
- **Eligibility:** 2 to 4 unmarried Singapore Citizens applying together.
- **Age Requirement:** All applicants must be at least 35 years old.
- **Eligible Flat Types:** 2-Room Flexi flats in any BTO project (Standard, Plus, Prime).
- **Income Ceiling:** Combined gross monthly income cannot exceed $14,000.

## 4. General Eligibility Conditions & Restrictions
- **Citizenship:** At least one Singapore Citizen (SC) applicant.
- **Age:** Minimum 21 years old for families/couples, minimum 35 years old for singles.
- **Private Property Ownership:**
  - Applicants and occupiers must not currently own any private residential property (locally or overseas).
  - Must have disposed of any private property at least **30 months** prior to applying for an HFE letter / BTO flat.
- **Existing Subsidised Housing:** Each Singapore Citizen is entitled to buy a maximum of **two subsidised HDB flats** from the government (first-timer and second-timer privileges). Second-timers may need to pay a Resale Levy when purchasing a second subsidised flat.

## 5. CPF Housing Grants for BTO Flats

### Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG)
The Enhanced CPF Housing Grant is the primary housing grant available for new BTO flats.

- **For First-Timer Families:**
  - **Maximum Grant Amount:** Up to **$120,000** (increased from $80,000 in August 2024).
  - **Income Tiering:** The grant amount is inversely tiered based on average gross monthly household income assessed over 12 months:
    - Income $\le$ $1,500: $120,000 grant.
    - Income between $1,501 and $2,000: $110,000.
    - Income between $2,001 and $2,500: $100,000.
    - Tiered down to $5,000 for incomes up to $9,000.
  - **Household Income Ceiling:** Gross monthly household income must not exceed **$9,000**.
  - **Employment Condition:** At least one applicant must have worked continuously for at least 12 months prior to the flat application, and remain employed at the time of application.
  - **Flat Lease Condition:** Flat must have a remaining lease that covers the youngest applicant up to age 95; otherwise, the grant is prorated.

- **For First-Timer Singles (Single Singapore Citizen Scheme):**
  - **Maximum Grant Amount:** Up to **$60,000** (tiered according to personal monthly income).
  - **Income Ceiling:** Monthly income not exceeding **$4,500**.
  - **Employment Condition:** Continuously employed for at least 12 months.

### Step-Up CPF Housing Grant
- Available to second-timer lower-income families living in a 2-room subsidised flat who wish to buy a new 2-room or 3-room BTO flat in non-mature / standard areas.
- **Grant Amount:** Up to **$15,000**.

### Staggered Downpayment Scheme
- Allows eligible first-timer couples (e.g. students or NSFs, or young couples under the age of 30) to pay their downpayment in two tranches:
  - Tranche 1: When signing the Agreement for Lease (around 4 to 9 months after booking).
  - Tranche 2: At key collection when the flat is completed.

## 6. The HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) Letter Process
All home buyers must apply for and secure a valid **HFE letter** via the **HDB Flat Portal** before they can apply for a BTO sales launch.

### Key Steps:
1. **Step 1 - Preliminary HFE Check:**
   - Quick online check through the HDB Flat Portal to get an indicative view of eligibility for flats, CPF grants, and HDB housing loans.
2. **Step 2 - Formal HFE Letter Application:**
   - Submit personal particulars, income documents, and Singpass authentication.
   - HDB reviews the application within 21 working days (may take longer during peak BTO launch months).
3. **Validity Period:**
   - The approved HFE letter is valid for **9 months** from the date of issue.
   - Must be valid at the time of BTO application and flat booking.
4. **Deferred Income Assessment (DIA):**
   - Tailored for young couples (e.g., full-time students or National Servicemen) where one or both are not yet working or have worked less than 12 months.
   - Enables young couples to apply for a BTO flat first and defer their income assessment for the EHG grant and HDB housing loan until about 3 months before key collection.

## 7. BTO Application, Booking, and Timeline
1. **Sales Launch Announcement:** HDB holds BTO launches three times a year (typically February, June, and October). Projects, flat mix, prices, and classifications (Standard, Plus, Prime) are published.
2. **Online Application:** Applicants submit an online application on the HDB Flat Portal within the 7-day launch window (valid HFE letter required).
3. **Ballot Results:** Ballot queue numbers are released approximately 1.5 to 2 months after launch.
4. **Flat Selection:** Successful balloters attend an appointment to choose their unit and pay an option fee ($500 for 2-Room Flexi, $1,000 for 3-Room, $2,000 for 4-Room and larger).
5. **Signing Agreement for Lease:** Around 4 to 9 months after booking, pay downpayment and stamp fees.
6. **Key Collection:** When construction completes (typically 3 to 5 years from launch).
